# Project overview

TeviAI Support Studio is an independent portfolio prototype for small-business customer support. It gives an owner or shift lead one place to classify a fictional enquiry, review risk warnings, draft a reply, make a decision and inspect the decision record. [Live demonstration](https://teviai-support-studio.streamlit.app/) · [Source repository](https://github.com/jthivyanp-rgb/teviai-support-studio)

**Problem.** Repeated questions about delivery, product availability and refunds take time. Some requests also involve privacy, legal risk, money or facts that staff need to verify. A tool that drafts text can help with the routine work while the business retains the decisions.

**Walkthrough.** Open the inbox, submit a fictional enquiry, create a rule-based draft and send it to the Approval Centre for review. There, choose a demo reviewer and approve, reject or escalate. Approved replies are available to copy. Activity Log shows the decisions and lets the reviewer export filtered metadata.

**Scope.** The site uses fictional business policies and session-only records. Rule-based drafting is the demonstrated public workflow. The optional Gemini adapter is covered by local test doubles. The evaluation measures controlled behaviours and includes no customer adoption or business-impact measurements.
